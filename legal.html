Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026  |  Indulgence & Nightlife

Indulgence & Nightlife ("IN", "we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store and share your data when you use our website at indulgenceandnightlife.com and related services.

1. Who We Are

Indulgence & Nightlife is operated by Engineuity Group Ltd, based in Auckland, New Zealand. For privacy enquiries, contact us at: hello@indulgenceandnightlife.com

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Email address and name — when you sign up to our mailing list
  • Payment information — processed securely by Stripe; we never store your card details
  • Shipping address — when you place an order, collected by Stripe and passed to Printful for fulfilment
  • Usage data — pages visited, time spent, clicks and interactions via Microsoft Clarity
  • Device information — browser type, operating system, IP address
  • Community content — any content you submit for inclusion on the IN platform

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To fulfil your orders and process payments
  • To send you updates, newsletters and promotional content (with your consent)
  • To improve our website and understand how visitors use it
  • To manage your membership and community participation
  • To comply with legal obligations
  • To prevent fraud and ensure platform security

4. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, we process your personal data under the following legal bases:

  • Contract — to fulfil orders you place with us
  • Consent — for marketing emails and non-essential cookies
  • Legitimate interests — for website analytics and fraud prevention
  • Legal obligation — where required by law

5. Third Parties We Share Data With

  • Stripe — payment processing (stripe.com/privacy)
  • Printful — order fulfilment and shipping (printful.com/privacy-policy)
  • Mailchimp (Intuit) — email marketing (mailchimp.com/legal/privacy)
  • Microsoft Clarity — website behaviour analytics (privacy.microsoft.com)

We do not sell your personal data to any third party.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as necessary to provide our services and comply with legal obligations. Email marketing data is retained until you unsubscribe. Order data is retained for 7 years for tax and accounting purposes.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

  • Right to access the personal data we hold about you
  • Right to correct inaccurate data
  • Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten")
  • Right to restrict or object to processing
  • Right to data portability
  • Right to withdraw consent at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at hello@indulgenceandnightlife.com. We will respond within 30 days.

8. New Zealand Privacy Act 2020

We comply with the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020. You have the right to access and correct personal information we hold about you. To make a request, contact us at the email above. If you are unsatisfied with our response, you may contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner at privacy.org.nz.

9. International Data Transfers

Your data may be transferred to and processed in countries outside New Zealand, including the United States, where our service providers are based. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place in accordance with applicable privacy laws.

10. Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, loss or destruction. All payments are processed over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS).

11. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed at children under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe we have collected data from a child, please contact us immediately.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by email or by posting a notice on our website. The date at the top of this page indicates when it was last updated.

13. Contact Us

For any privacy-related questions or concerns: hello@indulgenceandnightlife.com
